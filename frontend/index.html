Caught early, it costs $1 to cure.

Noma is a gangrenous infection that can destroy a child's face within days. It strikes children aged 2–6 living in extreme poverty, most of them in sub-Saharan Africa, and the great majority of cases are never detected in time.

lumos.health gives community health workers an AI triage tool: photograph the wound, get a WHO-staged assessment and a clinic referral in seconds.
